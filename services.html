MEDICAL & COMMUNITY SERVICES

Detailed Care Options Across Home Health, Personal Assistance, and Community Living.

The categories below are modeled on the service range of established medical home-care and waiver providers, with original wording for Amazing Health Care Services LLC. Only publish a service as actively available once the agency confirms it is licensed, enrolled, credentialed, and authorized to provide it.

PCA

Personal Care Aide (PCA) Services

Hands-on assistance with activities of daily living, personal routines, and approved household tasks.

Personal Care Aide services support individuals who need help completing routine activities of daily living while remaining in their own home or community setting.

READ DETAILED SERVICE PAGE →
HHA

Home Health Aide (HHA) Services

Personal and health-related assistance provided as part of an authorized home health plan and under appropriate supervision.

Home Health Aide services can provide hands-on assistance for individuals whose care plan includes home health aide support under the direction or supervision required for the service.

READ DETAILED SERVICE PAGE →
RN

Skilled Nursing Services

Licensed nursing care for individuals who require clinical assessment, monitoring, treatment, or other ordered nursing interventions at home.

Skilled Nursing services are designed for patients who need clinical care that must be provided by a licensed nurse within an authorized plan of care.

READ DETAILED SERVICE PAGE →
R

Respite Care

Temporary care that gives a family caregiver time to rest, work, attend appointments, or manage other responsibilities.

Respite Care provides planned, temporary relief to a primary caregiver while maintaining safe and consistent support for the individual receiving services.

READ DETAILED SERVICE PAGE →
HCBS

Waiver & Community-Based Services

Person-centered services that may be available through Medicaid waiver or other community-based programs when the agency is enrolled and the individual is authorized.

Waiver and Home- and Community-Based Services help eligible individuals receive supports in community settings rather than relying only on institutional care.

READ DETAILED SERVICE PAGE →
C

Companion Services

Social, recreational, community, and routine IADL support that helps reduce isolation and promote participation.

Companion services provide fellowship, community participation, and routine support that helps individuals stay engaged and connected.

READ DETAILED SERVICE PAGE →
BS

Behavioral & Supportive Services

Structured support for routines, communication, behavior plans, coping strategies, and daily stability when authorized and appropriate.

Behavioral and supportive services are intended to help individuals participate more successfully in daily routines and community life when additional structured support is needed.

READ DETAILED SERVICE PAGE →
SL

Supported Living & Transportation

Residential and community support designed to build independence, daily-living skills, and access to authorized activities and services.

Supported Living services help eligible individuals develop and maintain skills needed to live successfully in a community residence with an appropriate level of support.

READ DETAILED SERVICE PAGE →
IHS

In-Home Supports

Habilitation, personal support, skill building, and other authorized assistance delivered in the individual’s home.

In-Home Supports are designed to help eligible individuals build or maintain skills and remain successful in their own home or family home.

READ DETAILED SERVICE PAGE →
DAY

Individualized Day Services

Individualized community and day activities focused on participation, skill development, choice, and meaningful routines.

Individualized Day Services provide person-centered support during the day based on the individual’s interests, needs, and goals rather than a one-size-fits-all schedule.

READ DETAILED SERVICE PAGE →
HOME

Host Home / Residential Support

Community-based residential support in a qualified home setting for individuals who require ongoing assistance and supervision.

Host Home or comparable residential support models provide a community-based living arrangement where an individual receives structured support in a home environment.
