Home Health Aide (HHA) Services

Home Health Aide services can provide hands-on assistance for individuals whose care plan includes home health aide support under the direction or supervision required for the service.

Service availability is subject to agency licensure, enrollment, authorization, staffing, payer requirements, and the individual plan of care.
ASK ABOUT THIS SERVICE →

How This Service Works

An HHA may assist with personal care, hygiene, mobility, positioning, routine observations, and other authorized tasks that support the patient’s health and daily functioning. Services should be based on the established plan and remain within the aide’s training and permitted scope.

Home Health Aides are expected to communicate concerns to the supervising clinician, follow infection-prevention practices, observe for changes that may affect care, and document completed tasks and observations according to agency procedures.

HHA services work best when the aide, nurse, patient, family, and other authorized care team members communicate clearly about routines, changes, and goals.

Common Components

  • Personal hygiene assistance
  • Mobility and positioning support
  • Routine observations
  • Support with ordered home-care routines
  • Communication with supervising clinicians
  • Documentation of services

Planning, Documentation, and Coordination

Services should follow the applicable medical order, authorization, person-centered plan, plan of care, or service agreement. Staff document the support provided, communicate significant changes, and remain within the scope of the role for which they are trained and authorized.
