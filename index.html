Healthcare and Support Designed Around the Whole Person.

Amazing Health Care Services LLC brings together licensed clinical care, personal assistance, and community-based services for individuals who need structured support to remain safe, stable, and engaged in everyday life.

Our service model may include Skilled Nursing, Home Health Aide care, Personal Care Aide services, respite, waiver supports, companion services, behavioral support, in-home services, supported living, individualized day services, and residential support when the agency is properly authorized to provide them.

THE CARE TEAM

Clear Roles. Clear Responsibilities. Better Coordination.

Medical and community-based care may involve multiple professionals and direct-support roles. A Registered Nurse has different responsibilities from a Home Health Aide, Personal Care Aide, Direct Support Professional, or companion.

Our care model emphasizes appropriate scope, supervision, documentation, and communication so each team member understands what they are authorized and trained to do.

MEET THE CARE TEAM
Registered NursesClinical assessment, monitoring, treatment, education, and coordination.
Home Health AidesAuthorized personal and health-related support under required supervision.
Personal Care AidesADL support and approved personal-assistance tasks.
Direct Support ProfessionalsHabilitation, community participation, residential, and skill-building support.
FOR REFERRAL PARTNERS

A Clear Path From Referral to Service Review.

Hospitals, physicians, case managers, support coordinators, families, and community partners can contact our office to discuss a potential referral. The exact intake process depends on the service requested.

1Send the referral

Share the individual’s contact information, needs, requested services, and available clinical or program documentation.

2Service review

The agency reviews scope, eligibility, authorization requirements, staffing, and whether the requested service can be safely provided.

3Planning and start of care

If accepted, the appropriate assessment, plan, authorization, staffing, and start-of-service steps are completed.

Need to Refer Someone?
