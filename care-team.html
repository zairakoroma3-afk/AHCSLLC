OUR CARE TEAM

Different Roles Working Toward One Person-Centered Plan.

Staffing depends on the service, program, patient needs, agency credentials, and authorization. The roles below describe the types of professionals commonly used across medical home health and community-based care.

Registered Nurses

Provide licensed nursing assessment, monitoring, treatment, education, and care coordination within professional scope and ordered plans of care.

Home Health Aides

Support personal care and authorized health-related routines under the supervision required for the home health service.

Personal Care Aides

Assist with activities of daily living and other authorized personal-assistance tasks based on the individual plan.

Direct Support Professionals

Provide habilitation, skill building, community participation, residential, and person-centered supports within authorized programs.

Medication-Trained Staff

Where permitted and properly authorized, trained staff may support medication routines according to applicable rules, delegation, and program requirements.

Therapy & Clinical Partners

Physical, occupational, speech-language, nurse practitioner, physician, and other clinical professionals may coordinate with the agency when included in the individual’s authorized care team.
